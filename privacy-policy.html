1. Introduction

Bizecurity ("we," "our," or "us") is a cybersecurity application designed to help small businesses protect their digital assets, monitor threats, and maintain security best practices.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Bizecurity mobile application ("App") and related services ("Services").

By downloading, installing, or using Bizecurity, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration information (name, email address, business name)
  • Profile and business configuration settings
  • Support requests and communications with our team
  • Payment information (processed by third-party providers; we do not store full card numbers)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • App usage data and feature interactions
  • Log data including IP addresses, access times, and error reports
  • Security scan results and threat detection data generated by the App

2.3 Business Security Data

As a cybersecurity application, Bizecurity may collect and process information related to your business's security posture, including:

  • Network configuration details you choose to submit for analysis
  • Security audit results and vulnerability assessment data
  • Incident reports and security event logs

This data is used solely to provide security services and is treated with the highest level of confidentiality.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Bizecurity App and Services
  • Process your account registration and authenticate your identity
  • Deliver security monitoring, threat alerts, and vulnerability reports
  • Improve and develop new features and functionality
  • Respond to customer support inquiries
  • Send transactional emails and security alerts (you may opt out of marketing communications)
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or unauthorized access

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a MongoDB database hosted on Railway (railway.app), a secure cloud infrastructure provider. Our backend API is built with FastAPI/Python and deployed on Railway's production environment.

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls limiting employee access to your data
While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
